# Clinic access vocabulary

This glossary describes the Mayaana clinic access context and the vocabulary used by the current clinic product.

## Language

**Clinic**:
The practice operated by one doctor, with one secretary initially and the possibility of more secretaries later.

**Doctor account**:
The sign-in identity for the clinic's doctor, provisioned by the system administrator.

**Secretary account**:
The sign-in identity for a secretary, created and controlled by the doctor.

**Login email**:
The unique email address identifying a person at sign-in. Call or WhatsApp contact numbers may be associated with that person later and are separate from the login identity.
_Avoid_: Contact number, login phone number

**Temporary password**:
A password supplied when an account is created or reset, which its owner must replace at their next sign-in.

**Patient**:
The person receiving care, whose own app and login are shown in later V3 Figma frames and are outside the first clinic Login Flow.
